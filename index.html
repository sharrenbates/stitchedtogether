Warm hands, warm neighbors

Every winter, cold hits the Delaware River towns hardest for the people who already have the least buffer against it. Stitched Together started as a handful of neighbors with needles, yarn, and an afternoon to spare — sewing and knitting hats, mittens, scarves, and other cold-weather gear for anyone in New Hope, Lambertville, and the surrounding area who needs it.

No one is asked to prove they need what we make. If you're cold, it's yours.

In grateful partnership with Fisherman's Mark.

What we make

Hats, mittens, scarves, and whatever else the season calls for — all handmade, all given away.

Sunday Stitch meetups

Bring a project — or borrow one of ours. We gather around town in New Hope and Lambertville to sew, knit, and crochet together. Bring your needles or use one of ours, beginners are always welcome, and you're welcome to just sit and wind yarn if that's more your speed.

Meetup locations move around, so follow us on Instagram or Facebook for this week's time and place.
